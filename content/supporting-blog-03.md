## Introduction

Getting hair extensions is only the beginning. Proper aftercare plays an important role in keeping extensions manageable and maintaining their appearance.

Whether you have tape-ins, keratin bonds, rings, wefts or another extension system, your maintenance routine should match the specific method used.

If you are considering **hair extensions in Bangalore**, understanding the care involved can help you decide whether extensions fit your lifestyle.

## Why Is Hair Extension Maintenance Important?

Hair extensions are attached to your natural hair and require different care from untreated natural hair.

Poor maintenance can contribute to:

- Tangling
- Matting
- Excessive pulling
- Difficulty brushing
- Damage to the extensions
- Problems around attachment points

Following professional aftercare instructions can make your extension routine easier to manage.

## 1. Brush Hair Extensions Gently

Brushing should be done carefully to reduce unnecessary pulling.

### Recommended approach

1. Separate the hair into manageable sections.
2. Start detangling from the ends.
3. Gradually work upward.
4. Hold the hair near the attachment area when brushing close to it.
5. Avoid aggressively pulling through knots.

Use a brush appropriate for your extension system.

## 2. Wash Hair Carefully

Your washing routine may need to change after getting extensions.

### Before washing

Gently detangle the hair.

### During washing

- Use products recommended for your extension system.
- Avoid rough scrubbing around attachment points.
- Allow shampoo to cleanse the scalp and hair without aggressive rubbing.
- Rinse thoroughly.

Your stylist should explain exactly which products and techniques are suitable for your extensions.

## 3. Dry Hair Thoroughly

Leaving hair damp for long periods may make it harder to manage.

After washing:

- Gently squeeze out excess water.
- Avoid aggressive towel rubbing.
- Dry the hair according to your stylist's instructions.
- Pay attention to the attachment areas.

Different extension systems may have different drying requirements.

## 4. Use Heat Carefully

Frequent high-temperature styling can affect extension hair.

If heat styling is appropriate for your extensions:

- Use suitable heat protection.
- Avoid unnecessarily high temperatures.
- Keep excessive heat away from attachment points.
- Follow your stylist's instructions.

## 5. Sleep With Hair Managed

Hair can become tangled during sleep because of friction and movement.

Depending on your extension system, your stylist may recommend:

- Gently detangling before bed
- Keeping hair loosely secured
- Using a suitable pillowcase
- Avoiding sleeping with heavily tangled hair

Ask your stylist which method is appropriate for your extensions.

## 6. Be Careful When Exercising

Sweat and frequent movement can make hair more difficult to manage.

Before exercise, consider securing your hair gently to reduce tangling.

After exercising, follow your normal cleansing and drying routine when needed.

Avoid hairstyles that create excessive pulling around attachment points.

## 7. Follow the Professional Maintenance Schedule

Longer-wear extension systems usually require professional maintenance.

The schedule can depend on:

- Extension method
- Natural hair growth
- Hair condition
- Home care
- Extension placement

Do not assume that all extension types have the same maintenance schedule.

## 8. Avoid Excessive Pulling

Tight hairstyles can create unnecessary tension.

Be particularly careful with:

- Very tight ponytails
- Tight buns
- Aggressive brushing
- Pulling at attachment areas

Choose comfortable hairstyles and follow professional recommendations.

## 9. Choose Hair Products Carefully

Not every hair product is appropriate for every extension system.

Ask your stylist about:

- Shampoo
- Conditioner
- Hair masks
- Oils
- Leave-in products
- Heat protectants
- Styling products

Product recommendations can vary depending on the extension method and attachment system.

## 10. Schedule Professional Checks

A professional can assess the condition of the extensions and attachment areas.

Regular checks can help identify issues such as:

- Excessive tangling
- Loose attachments
- Matting
- Uncomfortable tension
- Need for maintenance

Follow the schedule recommended for your particular extension system.

## Common Hair Extension Care Mistakes

### Brushing Too Aggressively

Pulling through knots quickly can place unnecessary tension on the hair.

### Sleeping With Wet Hair

Wet hair can be more difficult to manage and may increase tangling.

### Using Excessive Heat

High heat can affect extension hair and may make it dry or difficult to manage.

### Ignoring Maintenance

Waiting too long for professional maintenance can make extensions increasingly difficult to manage.

### Using Products Without Checking Compatibility

Some products may not be appropriate for particular extension systems.

Always check with your stylist.

## A Simple Hair Extension Care Routine

### Daily

### Professional Maintenance

Follow the schedule provided by your stylist.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should I brush hair extensions?

The frequency depends on your hair and extension system. Gentle regular detangling can help prevent knots, but your stylist can recommend a routine suitable for your extensions.

### Can I wash hair extensions?

Many extension systems can be washed, but the technique and products depend on the method. Follow your stylist's instructions.

### Can I use heat on hair extensions?

Some extensions can be heat-styled. Use appropriate heat protection and avoid excessive heat, especially around attachment points.

### Can I sleep with hair extensions?

Longer-wear extensions are designed to remain in place, but hair should be managed carefully before sleeping to reduce tangling.

### Why are my hair extensions tangling?

Tangling can result from friction, insufficient detangling, improper washing, dryness, extension condition or delayed maintenance.

### When should I get professional maintenance?

The correct interval depends on the extension method and natural hair growth. Follow the schedule provided by your stylist.

Gentle brushing, careful washing, appropriate drying, controlled heat styling and professional maintenance can help keep extensions manageable.
