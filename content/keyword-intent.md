**Commercial Investigation**

They may want to understand:

- Different types of hair extensions
- Expected cost factors
- Hair extension maintenance
- Suitability for different hair types
- How to choose a professional salon
- What to know before booking a service

## Search Intent Breakdown

### Informational Intent

The user wants to learn something.

Examples:

- Types of hair extensions
- Hair extension maintenance
- Hair extensions vs wigs

**Best content format:** Educational blog articles.

### Commercial Investigation Intent

The user is researching options before making a decision.

Examples:

- Hair extensions in Bangalore
- Hair extension cost in Bangalore
- Choosing the right hair extensions

**Best content format:** Detailed guides, comparisons and service-focused articles.

### Transactional Intent

The user is closer to taking action.

Examples:

- Hair extension salon in Bangalore
- Hair extension services in Bangalore

**Best content format:** Service pages, consultation pages and strong calls to action.
