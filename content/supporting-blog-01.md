## Introduction

If you're considering hair extensions in Bangalore, one of the first questions you may have is: **How much will it cost?**

There isn't one fixed price for every hair extension service. The final cost can vary depending on the type of extensions, quality and length of the hair, amount required, application method and maintenance needs.

Understanding these factors can help you compare options and plan your budget before booking a consultation.

## What Determines Hair Extension Cost in Bangalore?

### 1. Type of Hair Extension

Different extension methods require different application techniques and levels of professional work.

Common options include:

- Tape-in extensions
- Keratin bond extensions
- Micro or nano ring extensions
- Weft extensions
- Clip-in or other temporary extensions

The method recommended for you will depend on your natural hair and desired result.

### 2. Quality of Hair

Hair quality is another important factor.

Extensions made from higher-quality hair may have different pricing from other options because of their appearance, texture and longevity.

When comparing prices, ask what type and quality of hair is included.

### 3. Length of Extensions

Longer extensions generally require more hair and may require additional application time.

If you want a significant increase in length, the amount of hair required can affect the overall cost.

### 4. Quantity of Hair Required

Someone looking for a small amount of added volume may require less hair than someone looking for substantial length and fullness.

A professional can determine the approximate quantity needed after assessing your natural hair.

### 5. Application Method

The application process varies between extension types.

Some methods require individual placement, while others use larger sections or panels.

The time and expertise required for application can therefore influence the total service cost.

## How Much Do Hair Extensions Cost at Salon Nayana?

Salon Nayana's official website currently lists hair extensions **from ₹25,000**.

However, this should not be treated as a universal price because the final requirement can vary from person to person.

Factors such as hair length, density, extension type, quantity and desired result can affect the final quote.

For the most accurate price, a consultation with the salon is recommended.

## Are Expensive Hair Extensions Always Better?

Not necessarily.

A higher price does not automatically mean that an extension method is the right choice for you.

Instead of comparing only prices, consider:

- Hair quality
- Application method
- Professional experience
- Maintenance requirements
- Expected longevity
- Suitability for your natural hair
- Removal process

The right choice should balance your desired result, hair requirements and budget.

## Questions to Ask Before Paying for Hair Extensions

Before confirming an appointment, ask:

1. What type of extensions are recommended for my hair?
2. What type of hair will be used?
3. How much hair will I need?
4. Is application included in the quoted price?
5. Is maintenance charged separately?
6. What is included in the package?
7. How are the extensions removed?
8. What aftercare products or routine are recommended?
9. What is the expected maintenance schedule?
10. What is the final estimated cost?

These questions can help prevent unexpected costs.

## How to Budget for Hair Extensions

Instead of considering only the initial application cost, think about the complete cost of maintaining the extensions.

Your overall budget may include:

**Initial application + maintenance + aftercare + eventual replacement or removal**

The exact requirements depend on the extension system you choose.

## Hair Extension Cost vs Value

The lowest price isn't always the best value.

A suitable extension system that is professionally applied and properly maintained may be more useful than choosing an option purely because it is inexpensive.

Consider the complete experience rather than just the initial price.
