## Introduction

Getting hair extensions can be an exciting way to explore a different hairstyle, add length or create additional volume.

However, making the decision without understanding the process can lead to unexpected maintenance, costs or styling requirements.

If you're searching for **hair extensions in Bangalore**, this guide covers the important questions to consider before booking an appointment.

## 1. Understand Your Goal

Start by deciding what you actually want from hair extensions.

Your goal could be:

- Adding length
- Adding volume
- Creating fuller-looking ends
- Changing your hairstyle
- Adding temporary length for an occasion
- Creating a different colour or dimension

Clearly communicating your goal can help your stylist understand what you're looking for.

## 2. Understand Your Natural Hair

Your natural hair plays an important role in choosing an extension method.

A professional may consider:

- Hair length
- Hair density
- Hair texture
- Hair condition
- Existing breakage
- Your usual hairstyles

Not every method is appropriate for every person.

## 3. Ask Which Method Is Suitable

There are several extension systems, including:

- Tape-ins
- Keratin bonds
- Micro or nano rings
- Wefts
- Clip-ins
- Halo extensions

Ask your stylist why a particular method is being recommended for your hair and lifestyle.

## 4. Ask About Hair Quality

Before confirming the service, ask what type of hair will be used.

Important questions include:

- What is the hair quality?
- How will the colour be matched?
- How will the texture be matched?
- What length is available?
- How should the hair be cared for?

Understanding what you are paying for makes it easier to compare options.

## 5. Ask About the Total Cost

Don't ask only about the starting price.

Ask for a breakdown that includes:

- Extension hair
- Application
- Colour matching, if required
- Maintenance
- Removal
- Any additional services

Salon Nayana's official website currently lists hair extensions from **₹25,000**, but individual requirements can affect the final cost.

Always confirm the current price directly with the salon before booking.

## 6. Understand the Maintenance Commitment

Longer-wear extensions require ongoing care.

Ask:

- How often is maintenance needed?
- How long does each appointment take?
- What happens during maintenance?
- Is maintenance included in the initial price?
- What happens if an attachment becomes loose?

Understanding maintenance beforehand can help you decide whether extensions fit your routine.

## 7. Learn the Aftercare Routine

Ask your stylist for specific instructions about:

- Washing
- Brushing
- Drying
- Heat styling
- Sleeping
- Exercise
- Hair products

Different extension systems can have different care requirements.

## 8. Ask About Removal

Before getting extensions, understand how they will eventually be removed.

Ask:

- Who should remove them?
- How long does removal take?
- Is removal included in the price?
- Can they be reused?
- What happens if they are left in for too long?

Professional removal is important for longer-wear extension systems.

## 9. Think About Your Lifestyle

Consider whether your everyday routine works with the extension method.

Think about:

- How often you wash your hair
- Exercise habits
- Travel
- Heat styling
- Hairstyles
- Time available for maintenance

The best option isn't necessarily the one that looks best in a photograph. It should also fit your lifestyle.

## 10. Consider the Long-Term Commitment

Hair extensions aren't always a one-time decision.

Depending on the method, you may need:

- Regular maintenance
- Special care products
- Professional appointments
- Eventual removal
- Replacement hair

Think about the full commitment before deciding.

## 11. Don't Choose Only Based on Price

A low price can be attractive, but it shouldn't be the only factor.

Compare:

- Hair quality
- Application method
- Professional experience
- Maintenance
- Aftercare
- Removal
- Overall value

A clear understanding of the service is more useful than comparing a single number.

## 12. Ask About Potential Discomfort

Extensions should be comfortable when appropriately selected and applied.

Ask your stylist what sensations are normal after application and what you should do if you experience significant discomfort.

Do not ignore persistent pain or excessive tension.

## 13. Plan Ahead for Important Events

If you're considering extensions for a wedding, photoshoot, vacation or other important event, avoid leaving the decision until the last moment.

Planning ahead allows time for:

- Consultation
- Colour matching
- Choosing a method
- Understanding maintenance
- Making adjustments if required

## 14. Consider a Professional Consultation

Online articles and photographs can help you research options, but they cannot assess your individual hair.

A professional consultation allows you to discuss:

- Your natural hair
- Desired result
- Extension method
- Hair quality
- Maintenance
- Cost
- Aftercare

This can help you make a more informed decision.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I consult a stylist before getting hair extensions?

Yes. A professional consultation can help determine whether a particular extension method is appropriate for your natural hair and desired result.

### How much do hair extensions cost in Bangalore?

Costs vary depending on the method, hair quality, length, quantity and application requirements. Salon Nayana currently lists hair extensions from ₹25,000, but the current individual price should be confirmed directly with the salon.

### How should I prepare for a hair extension appointment?

Discuss your desired result, natural hair condition, preferred method, budget and maintenance expectations with your stylist before the appointment.

### Can I continue my normal hairstyle after getting extensions?

This depends on the extension method and application. Ask your stylist which hairstyles are appropriate.

### How do I know which extension method is right for me?

A professional can assess your natural hair, desired result and lifestyle before recommending a suitable method.

### Should I get extensions immediately before a major event?

It's better to plan ahead so you have enough time for consultation, colour matching and understanding the maintenance routine.
