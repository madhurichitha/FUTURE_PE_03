# Pillar Blog Generation Prompt

## Purpose

This prompt generates a comprehensive, SEO-optimized pillar blog for a local business website.

## Prompt

Act as an experienced SEO content writer and local SEO strategist.

Write a comprehensive pillar blog for the following business:

**Business:** Salon Nayana  
**Location:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India  
**Business Type:** Hair & Beauty Salon  
**Main Topic:** Hair Extensions

### Primary Keyword

hair extensions in Bangalore

### Blog Title

**Hair Extensions in Bangalore: Types, Cost, Maintenance & How to Choose the Right One**

## Search Intent

The article should satisfy commercial investigation intent by helping readers understand hair extensions, compare options, consider costs and maintenance, and decide what factors to consider before choosing a professional salon.

## SEO Requirements

Create:

- SEO-friendly title
- Meta description
- URL slug
- One clear H1
- Logical H2 and H3 headings
- Natural primary keyword usage
- Relevant secondary and long-tail keywords
- FAQ section
- Internal linking opportunities
- Relevant call to action

## Suggested Structure

### H1
Hair Extensions in Bangalore: Types, Cost, Maintenance & How to Choose the Right One

### H2 Sections

1. Introduction
2. What Are Hair Extensions?
3. Types of Hair Extensions
4. How to Choose the Right Hair Extensions
5. How Much Do Hair Extensions Cost in Bangalore?
6. How Long Do Hair Extensions Last?
7. How to Maintain Hair Extensions
8. Common Mistakes to Avoid
9. What to Consider Before Getting Hair Extensions
10. Why Professional Consultation Matters
11. Frequently Asked Questions
12. Conclusion and Call to Action

Use H3 subheadings wherever they improve readability and organization.

## Writing Requirements

- Write for humans first and search engines second.
- Provide genuinely useful information.
- Use simple, clear and engaging language.
- Keep the tone professional, approachable and trustworthy.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Use the primary keyword naturally.
- Include related terminology naturally.
- Use short paragraphs and scannable sections.
- Add practical tips where appropriate.
- Answer common user questions directly.

## Local SEO Requirements

Naturally incorporate relevant local context such as:

- Bangalore
- Bengaluru
- Hair extensions in Bangalore
- Choosing a hair salon in Bangalore

Do not repeat location keywords unnaturally.

## Internal Linking

Suggest natural places where this pillar article can link to supporting articles in the content cluster.

Supporting topics include:

- Hair extension cost in Bangalore
- Types of hair extensions
- Hair extension maintenance
- Hair extensions vs hair wigs
- What to know before getting hair extensions

Use descriptive anchor text rather than generic phrases such as "click here."

## Accuracy & Safety

Do not invent:

- Customer reviews
- Testimonials
- Exact business prices
- Discounts or offers
- Certifications
- Search volume
- Ranking guarantees
- Statistics without reliable sources
- Business-specific claims that have not been verified

If discussing prices, explain that costs vary depending on factors such as extension type, hair quality, length, quantity and salon.

Do not make medical claims or guarantee a particular result.

## Business Goal

The article should help the business:

- Attract relevant organic traffic
- Build topical authority
- Educate potential customers
- Build trust
- Encourage qualified enquiries
- Support appointment or consultation requests

## Reusability

The prompt should be reusable for other local businesses.

The following variables should be replaceable:

- Business name
- Location
- Business type
- Main service
- Primary keyword
- Search intent
- Target audience
- Blog topic

## Expected Output

Return the completed pillar article with:

1. SEO title
2. Meta description
3. URL slug
4. H1-H3 structure
5. Long-form article
6. FAQ section
7. Internal linking suggestions
8. Call to action
