# Pillar Blog Generation Prompt

## Purpose

This prompt generates a comprehensive, SEO-optimized pillar blog for a local business website.

## Prompt

Act as an experienced SEO content writer and local SEO strategist.

Write a comprehensive pillar blog for the following business:

**Business:** Salon Nayana  
**Location:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India  
**Business Type:** Hair & Beauty Salon  
**Main Topic:** Hair Extensions

### Primary Keyword

hair extensions in Bangalore

### Blog Title

**Hair Extensions in Bangalore: Types, Cost, Maintenance & How to Choose the Right One**

## Search Intent

The article should satisfy commercial investigation intent by helping readers understand hair extensions, compare options, consider costs and maintenance, and decide what factors to consider before choosing a professional salon.

## SEO Requirements

Create:

- SEO-friendly title
- Meta description
- URL slug
- One clear H1
- Logical H2 and H3 headings
- Natural primary keyword usage
- Relevant secondary and long-tail keywords
- FAQ section
- Internal linking opportunities
- Relevant call to action

## Suggested Structure

### H1
Hair Extensions in Bangalore: Types, Cost, Maintenance & How to Choose the Right One

### H2 Sections

1. Introduction
2. What Are Hair Extensions?
3. Types of Hair Extensions
4. How to Choose the Right Hair Extensions
5. How Much Do Hair Extensions Cost in Bangalore?
6. How Long Do Hair Extensions Last?
7. How to Maintain Hair Extensions
8. Common Mistakes to Avoid
9. What to Consider Before Getting Hair Extensions
10. Why Professional Consultation Matters
11. Frequently Asked Questions
12. Conclusion and Call to Action

Use H3 subheadings wherever they improve readability and organization.

## Writing Requirements

- Write for humans first and search engines second.
- Provide genuinely useful information.
- Use simple, clear and engaging language.
- Keep the tone professional, approachable and trustworthy.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Use the primary keyword naturally.
