# Supporting Blog Generation Prompt

## Purpose

This prompt generates supporting SEO articles that strengthen the main pillar page and create a connected content cluster.

## Prompt

Act as an experienced SEO content writer and content cluster strategist.

Create a helpful supporting blog for the following business:

**Business:** Salon Nayana  
**Location:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India  
**Business Type:** Hair & Beauty Salon  
**Main Topic:** Hair Extensions

### Pillar Topic

**Hair Extensions in Bangalore: Types, Cost, Maintenance & How to Choose the Right One**

### Supporting Blog Topic

[INSERT SUPPORTING BLOG TOPIC]

### Target Keyword

[INSERT PRIMARY KEYWORD]

## Search Intent

Identify the search intent of the supporting topic and ensure the article directly satisfies what the user is looking for.

## SEO Requirements

Create:

- SEO title
- Meta description
- URL slug
- One clear H1
- Logical H2 and H3 headings
- Primary keyword usage
- Relevant secondary keywords
- Long-tail keyword opportunities
- FAQ section
- Internal linking recommendations
- Relevant call to action

## Article Structure

Use the following structure:

### H1
[Supporting Blog Title]

### Introduction

Briefly introduce the user's problem or question and explain what the article will help them understand.

### Main Sections

Organize the article using relevant H2 and H3 headings.

Cover the topic comprehensively while avoiding unnecessary repetition of information already covered in the pillar article.

### FAQ

Answer common questions related to the supporting topic.

### Conclusion

Summarize the key points and provide a useful next step for the reader.

## Content Cluster Linking

The supporting article must connect naturally to the pillar article.

Include:

- At least one contextual link to the pillar article.
- Additional links to other relevant supporting articles when appropriate.
- Descriptive anchor text.

The pillar article is:

**Hair Extensions in Bangalore: Types, Cost, Maintenance & How to Choose the Right One**

Supporting topics include:

1. How Much Do Hair Extensions Cost in Bangalore?
2. Types of Hair Extensions: Which One Is Right for You?
3. How to Maintain Hair Extensions: A Complete Care Guide
4. Hair Extensions vs Hair Wigs: Which Should You Choose?
5. What to Know Before Getting Hair Extensions in Bangalore?

## Local SEO

Use Bengaluru/Bangalore naturally when relevant.

Do not force location keywords into every section.

Focus on satisfying the reader's intent rather than keyword stuffing.

## Writing Requirements

- Write for people first.
- Use clear and natural language.
- Keep paragraphs concise.
- Provide practical information.
- Maintain a professional and approachable tone.
- Avoid repetitive content.
- Match the article closely to its search intent.
- Make the content useful as a standalone article.

## Accuracy Guidelines

Do not invent:

- Customer testimonials
- Reviews
- Prices
- Discounts
- Business-specific claims
- Certifications
- Statistics
- Search volume
- Ranking guarantees

If information requires verification, clearly indicate that it should be checked before publication.

Do not make medical claims or guarantee specific results.

## Business Goals

The article should help:

- Increase relevant organic traffic
- Strengthen topical authority
- Educate potential customers
- Build trust
- Support qualified enquiries
- Guide readers toward relevant salon services

## Reusability

The prompt should work for different businesses and topics.

Replaceable variables include:

- Business name
- Location
- Business type
- Main topic
- Supporting topic
- Primary keyword
- Search intent
- Target audience

## Expected Output

Return:

1. SEO title
2. Meta description
3. URL slug
4. H1-H3 outline
5. Complete supporting article
6. FAQ section
7. Internal linking suggestions
8. Call to action
