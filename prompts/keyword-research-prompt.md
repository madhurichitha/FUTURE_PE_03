# SEO Keyword & Search Intent Research Prompt

## Purpose

This prompt is designed to identify relevant SEO keywords, search intent and content opportunities for a local business.

## Prompt

Act as an experienced SEO keyword research specialist.

Analyze the following business and topic:

**Business:** Salon Nayana  
**Location:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India  
**Business Type:** Hair & Beauty Salon  
**Main Topic:** Hair Extensions

### Primary Keyword

hair extensions in Bangalore

### Tasks

Identify and organize:

1. Primary keyword
2. Secondary keywords
3. Long-tail keywords
4. Local SEO keywords
5. Question-based keywords
6. Informational keywords
7. Commercial investigation keywords
8. Transactional keywords
9. Related semantic keywords
10. Potential content topics

For every important keyword, explain:

- Search intent
- Likely user need
- Recommended content type
- Whether it should be targeted by the pillar article or a supporting article

### Content Cluster Planning

Group the keywords into logical topic clusters.

Create:

- One primary pillar topic
- 3–5 supporting topics
- Suggested internal links between the articles

### Local SEO

Include natural variations involving:

- Bangalore
- Bengaluru
- Hair extensions in Bangalore
- Hair extension services in Bangalore

Avoid keyword stuffing and avoid creating unnatural keyword variations.

### Accuracy Guidelines

Do not invent:

- Search volume numbers
- Keyword difficulty scores
- Rankings
- Customer data
- Business claims

If a metric requires a dedicated SEO tool, state that it should be verified using an SEO research platform.

### Expected Output

Return the results in a structured format containing:

**Keyword → Search Intent → User Need → Content Type → Recommended Article**

Then provide the final content cluster and internal linking recommendations.
